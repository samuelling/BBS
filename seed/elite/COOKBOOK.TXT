 RECIPE #1: COURIER NACHOS
  - one bag of tortilla chips, the cheap kind
  - a lot of shredded cheddar. More than that.
  - pickled jalapenos from the jar in the door of the fridge
  Microwave 90 seconds. Eat at keyboard. Do not get cheese on the 3.5".

 RECIPE #2: THE 0-DAY SANDWICH
  - peanut butter, potato chips, white bread
  Smash the chips into the PB. Ready before your download finishes.
