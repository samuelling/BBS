HOW NOT TO BE A LAMER IN THE MESSAGE BASES
------------------------------------------

 1. Read before you post. Somebody already asked.
 2. Don't type in ALL CAPS. We can hear you fine.
 3. Quote only what you're replying to, not the whole message.
 4. Put something real in the subject line. "HELP!!!" is not a subject.
 5. Keep flame wars out of General. Take it to E-mail.
 6. Say thanks when someone helps you. It costs nothing.
 7. Don't post the same message in every base.
 8. Upload something now and then. A board is only as good as its users.
 9. There is one phone line. Log off when you're done.

Be excellent to each other. Party on.
