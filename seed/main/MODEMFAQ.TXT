MODEM FAQ (the short version)
-----------------------------

Q: What do all those noises mean when I connect?
A: The two modems are introducing themselves. First a 2100 Hz answer
   tone, then they probe the line and agree on the fastest speed it
   can handle. The hiss at the end is them training their equalizers.

Q: Why do I keep getting dropped?
A: Usually call waiting. Dial *70, before the number to turn it off
   for the call. Or somebody picked up the extension. MOM.

Q: What does ATZ do?
A: Resets the modem to its saved settings. ATDT dials with tones.
   ATH0 hangs up. +++ gets the modem's attention mid-call.

Q: Zmodem, Ymodem or Xmodem?
A: Zmodem. It's faster and it can resume a broken download.

Q: Why is the line always busy?
A: There's one line. Try again in a few minutes.
